OrchestrAI reads a messy request the moment it arrives, decides what should happen, scores the risk of doing it, and only stops to ask a person when the action is genuinely risky. Everything it does is real — a ticket filed, an incident opened, a refund issued — and every decision is auditable, role-gated, and recoverable when something downstream fails.
